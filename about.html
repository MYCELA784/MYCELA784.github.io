About

Sourcing a part shouldn't take a day.

MYCELA is a parts sourcing layer for manufacturing MSMEs. We help buyers find the exact part they need and get it quoted by dealers who actually stock it.

The problem we started with

A bearing fails on a Tuesday afternoon. The maintenance person reads a designation off the housing, or tries to, because half the markings have worn away. Somebody calls three dealers. Two don't pick up. The third has a similar part but nobody is certain it's the right fit, so the machine waits.

Most industrial procurement in India still runs on phone calls, WhatsApp threads and personal relationships. That works, until the person you always call is unavailable and you have no structured way to find out who else stocks the part.

What MYCELA does

We hold a validated catalogue of industrial parts with the specifications that actually matter for fitment, and we let buyers search it three ways: by part number, by dimensions when the number is unreadable, and by equivalent across brands.

Once a buyer has found what they need, they build a single list and we route it to dealers. One request, one consolidated response, instead of one phone call per part.

  • We do not hold stock. Dealers own their inventory, pricing and delivery terms.
  • We do not sell listings. Buyers see the right part, not the part that paid the most.
  • We verify specifications. Every record in the catalogue is checked against the manufacturer's published data before it goes live.

Where we are today

Bearings are live, with linear motion and couplings in pilot. We add categories in the order buyers ask for them, which is why every search that returns nothing gets logged and reviewed. Fasteners and seals are next.
