For dealers

Enquiries from buyers who already know what they want.

We send you part enquiries with the designation, quantity and buyer details already confirmed. You quote. No listing fees, no subscription.

How it works

  • A buyer searches our catalogue and builds a list of the parts they need.
  • We check the enquiry is genuine and the buyer is contactable before it goes anywhere.
  • The list goes to dealers who stock those categories, including you.
  • You quote price and availability directly to the buyer. We stay out of the transaction.

What you get

Specified enquiries. The part number, quantity and application arrive with the request, so you're not spending twenty minutes on the phone working out what someone actually needs.

No listing fee. We don't charge for visibility and we don't rank dealers by payment. Early partners pay nothing while we prove the channel works.

Demand signal. Over time we can tell you what buyers in your area are searching for and not finding, which is useful for stocking decisions.

What we ask

  • Respond to enquiries within one working day, even if the answer is that you don't have it.
  • Quote honestly on availability. A confirmed "two weeks" beats an optimistic "tomorrow".
  • Tell us which categories and brands you actually stock, so we don't waste your time.

Who we're looking for right now

Bearing and power transmission dealers in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad. We're onboarding a small number of partners first so that every enquiry we send gets proper attention.
